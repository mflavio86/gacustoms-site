Frequently asked questions

Where does the information come from?
From the public General Aviation Airport Fact Sheets published by U.S. Customs and Border Protection (CBP). Each record links to the official PDF and shows the document date.

Is it official?
No. GA Customs Airports is an independent tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by CBP or any government agency. Records are transcribed automatically from the official PDFs. Always confirm hours, advance-notice requirements and procedures with CBP before you operate.

Does it work without internet?
Yes. Search, favorites and records work offline, and the map switches to a built-in basic map. To read a fact sheet PDF offline, open it once while online or use Settings → Offline library → Download.

A fact sheet says it can't be downloaded directly.
Some CBP fact sheets are not published with a direct PDF link. Tap “Open cbp.gov” to view them on the official CBP page.

I found an error.
Please email us the airport identifier and what is wrong. Corrections are published with app updates.
