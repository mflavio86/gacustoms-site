Privacy Policy

CBP Customs Airport · Effective October 27, 2026

CBP Customs Airport does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Data on your device

Your favorites, map preference and the fact sheet PDFs you download are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app removes them.

Free period (iCloud Keychain)

The app is free for 6 months from the first time you open it. To honor that period, the app saves two dates — when you first opened it and when you last used it — in your iCloud Keychain. Apple encrypts and syncs this item between your own devices; it is not sent to us and we cannot read it. It is kept after you delete the app, so that reinstalling does not restart the free period. The two dates are the only information stored there; they contain nothing that identifies you.

Purchases

The CBP Customs Airport Pro subscription is sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, Apple Account, payment details or any other personal data. The app only asks the App Store, on your device, whether your subscription is active. Manage or cancel it in your Apple Account settings.

Network requests

  • Fact sheet PDFs are downloaded directly from cbp.gov (U.S. Customs and Border Protection) when you open or download them. CBP's own privacy policy applies to those requests.
  • The online map is provided by Apple Maps (MapKit). Apple's privacy policy applies. The app does not request or use your location.

The app sends no identifiers with these requests and we do not operate any server that receives data from the app.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
